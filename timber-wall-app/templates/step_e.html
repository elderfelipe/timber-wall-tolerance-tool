Step E — As-Built Compliance Check

DIN 18202 Section 6 ("Compliance control") compares an actual, site-measured dimension against the permitted deviation - a workflow this app computes everywhere else but never let you actually use. Enter what you measured on site; everything else here is the design values already computed in Steps A/D.
