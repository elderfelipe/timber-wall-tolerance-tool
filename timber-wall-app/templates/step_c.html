Consolidated Compliance Summary

Panel-to-Panel Thickness Step (New in Step C)

Two adjacent sheets of the same nominal thickness can each land anywhere in their published thickness tolerance - the worst-case visible ridge at the seam is their combined tolerance, checked against DIN 18202's flatness limit at its tightest measuring distance (<0.1m), for your selected finish class.

Sensitivity Analysis — What's Actually Driving Each Result

One-at-a-time analysis: each contributing tolerance source is zeroed individually to see how much the RSS result would drop - the source with the largest drop is the one actually driving that number. Answers "would a better panel grade help more than straighter studs?" directly from the numbers.

Unverified flags mark tolerance values ambiguous in source extraction. Overall verdict uses RSS only (the more realistic combination) - worst-case figures are shown per-check for reference but don't drive the single pass/fail banner.
